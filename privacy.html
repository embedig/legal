Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Embedig Content Publisher ("the tool"), operated by Embedig, does with data from the platforms it connects to.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by Embedig to manage Embedig's own accounts. It is not available to the public.

Data we access

  • Account identifiers and basic profile (for example a TikTok open_id and display name, or a YouTube channel id and title), so we know which account a video goes to.
  • Access tokens issued by the platform after the account owner signs in and grants permission.
  • Videos and captions that we create and choose to upload.

We do not access followers' personal data, messages, or any data of other users.

How we store and protect it

Tokens are stored encrypted on Embedig's own computer and are used only to upload videos the owner has approved. We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while an account stays connected. Removing an account in the tool deletes its stored tokens. You can also revoke access at any time in your TikTok or Google account settings.
