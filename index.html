Embedig Content Publisher

Embedig Content Publisher is an internal publishing tool of Embedig, a small content company in Thailand. We use it to publish videos that we create ourselves to our own social media accounts, such as our YouTube channels and our TikTok account @warmpetlife.

How it works

  1. Our team prepares a video and its caption.
  2. The account owner reviews and approves every video before anything is sent to a platform.
  3. The tool uploads the approved video to the owner's own account through the platform's official API (for TikTok: as a draft that the owner finishes and posts in the TikTok app).

The tool is not offered to the public and only connects accounts owned by Embedig.
